- **New:** the dislocation allowance (DLA) for your pay band, with and without dependents, on the DLA step (90, 60 and 30 days out) and as a full table, O-10 to E-1, with the DLA form. These are DTMO's primary rates effective January 1, 2026, quoted and dated; the pay band picker is the same one the neighborhood pages use. A second DLA, paid only when orders are amended or canceled, has its own rates, which aren't shown.
- **New:** how the effective date of your PCS orders is figured (the day you report, minus the travel days you used, plus one day), from DTMO's worked example, on the move sequence page. Every step timed from your report or detachment date links to it; your weight allowance and DLA rate are set as of that date.
- **Why:** both were the last open gaps in the sequence's money rows.
