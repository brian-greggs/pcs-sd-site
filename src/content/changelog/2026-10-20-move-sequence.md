- **New:** `/sequence/` and one page per phase (orders in hand; 90, 60 and 30 days out; on the road; arrival; first 30 days): 43 steps in order, each with its timing, deadline, what has to come first, who it applies to, its forms, and the official pages it rests on, quoted and dated. `/sequence/forms/` lists 14 forms and claims with the deadline, where each is filed and what to attach.
- **Where the official pages disagree,** the page uses the newest authority and shows the older guidance it didn't use, with the reason. Where they leave something open, the step says "Not yet confirmed" and who to ask (your CPPA, your PPPO or the Housing Service Center). The housing control date shows both official rules side by side.
- **Scope:** these are Navy rules. Every sequence page says so, and so do the Camp Pendleton, MCAS Miramar and MCRD San Diego pages; Marine Corps steps aren't covered yet.
- **Starting plan (rules v0.9):** "The admin sequence" and "Move logistics" now lead with the sequence steps for your report window (under 30 days: arrival; 30–90 days: 90, 60 and 30 days out; 90 days or more, or no orders yet: orders in hand), filtered to your household. They used to fall back to your second priority.
- **Why:** families asked what to do first after orders and when each claim is due; the answers now come from the official pages, in order.
