- **PPM closeout:** the deadline stays 45 days after you complete the move (PPA), marked "Not yet confirmed" with your PPPO. A separate note covers Sailors who took a PPM advance: NAVSUP's 2023 handout gives them 45 days to send the package to Navy HHG Audit, and late paperwork can put pay into collection.
- **Where the Navy PPM package goes:** NAVSUP's page decides it (DPS for a PPM started on or after May 15, 2026; email for older ones). Its two conditions are worded differently ("started", "approved"), so the step and the form quote both and add a second "Not yet confirmed" line: ask Navy Household Goods, 855-444-6683.
- **Quick claims:** paid within five calendar days (PPA's October 2026 guide); the web page's "five business days" is shown as a source note.
- **Pets:** the JTR's limits (one cat or dog, up to $550 for a move within the U.S.) stay, confirmed.
