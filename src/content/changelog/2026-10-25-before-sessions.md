title: Fixes before the first user sessions
version: rules v0.10
---

- **Changed:** on the starting plan, choosing an answer no longer jumps to the next question. Next (or Enter) moves on, and it waits until the question is answered; "Not sure yet" counts as an answer, and pets can stay at none.
- **Changed:** moving on your own? Where the plan shows rent as a share of the housing allowance, it now says that the share uses the with-dependents rate, and, with a base chosen, shows your pay grade's rate without dependents beside it, with its year and date.
- **Changed:** the bedroom helper no longer says "the smallest row we have is 2 bedrooms". The rent ratios start at 2 bedrooms; every neighborhood page's rent table also lists studio and 1-bedroom rents. For officers and senior enlisted the helper says that CNIC lists no bedroom standard for them.
- **Changed:** on the home page, "What's sent?" opens under the question box instead of inside it, so it no longer covers the answer on a phone, and it shows the previous answer cut short with how long the full text is. Source links and the related page open in a new tab, so going back never loses the conversation.
- **Fixed:** an error is no longer kept as the previous turn, and your question stays in the box to try again; "New conversation" while an answer is on its way drops that answer. A question that takes over 45 seconds stops with a message.
- No figure, fact, deadline or source changed.
