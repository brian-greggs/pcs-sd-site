- **Changed:** base pages now work like neighborhood pages. On a phone, Off base and Base housing are short lists of groups you open one at a time: each drive-time group (under 30, 30–44, 45–59, 60+ minutes), the base housing tied to the base, and the areas not tied to a specific base. You can reach base housing without scrolling past 40 neighborhoods. On a computer, a list of those groups sits down the left side. The Off base / Base housing switch is gone.
