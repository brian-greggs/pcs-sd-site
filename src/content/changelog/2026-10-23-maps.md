title: Maps on the home page, every base page and every neighborhood page
version: v0.8.0

- **New:** the home page has a map of the eight bases and the 40 neighborhoods. Each neighborhood and base on it links to its page.
- **New:** each base page has a map of the neighborhoods shaded by their weekday 0700 drive to that base: under 30, 30–44, 45–59 and 60 minutes or more. These are the same groups as the lists below the map and the starting plan, and a neighborhood with several zips sits in the group for its slowest zip.
- **New:** each neighborhood page has a small map of where it is, with the nearest base by the 0700 drive.
- **How they're made:** the shapes are US Census Bureau boundaries (2020 zip areas; 2025 counties, freeways, military installations and water), downloaded 2026-10-22, with Mexico from Natural Earth. The maps are drawn when the site is built, so they load nothing from anywhere else and need no JavaScript. The lists and tables on each page still have every fact the map shows.
