- **New sources:** the Joint Travel Regulations (change effective October 1, 2026), the NAVADMIN 210/25 message itself, NAVSUP's Navy household goods and PPM pages, and the DoW Personal Property Activity's (ppa.mil) guides, all saved by hand on October 22. Where one of them is a higher authority than the page a step quoted before, the step now quotes it; where it says something different, the older wording moves to "Older guidance that says otherwise", with the reason.
- **New:** the household goods weight allowance by pay grade (JTR Table 5-37) on the forms page, after household goods counseling, with and without dependents, quoted and dated.
- **Changed:** PPM closeout: PPMs started on or after May 15, 2026 are uploaded and closed out in DPS (NAVSUP); the deadline is 45 days after you complete the move (PPA), still "Not yet confirmed, ask your PPPO". Pets: one cat or dog, up to $550 for a move within the U.S. (JTR). TLE: up to $290 a day. Receipts: any expense of $75 or more (JTR and DoD FMR). The travel claim goes through your CPPA via eCRM; NAVADMIN 210/25 cancels the MyPCS mandate. Quick claims, inconvenience claims and the 180-day notice now quote PPA's own guides. Dependent travel and household goods counseling are no longer marked "Not yet confirmed".
- **Restored:** the official line that first-time movers must see the transportation office before using DPS. Its wording includes a word our page-text check bans, so that check now skips verbatim official quotes in the quote blocks only, never page text.
- **Why:** the JTR and the Navy and DoD program offices' pages had been blocked to our scripts; with them saved, the sequence rests on the highest source that says each thing.
