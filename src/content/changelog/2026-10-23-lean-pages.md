title: Leaner pages, with every source one tap away
version: v0.9.0
---

- **Changed:** every section now opens with its one-line summary and stays closed until you tap it. Under each section, a small "Source" chip opens to where the facts come from, the dates they were checked and the license. The Sources table at the bottom of each page is still there.
- **Changed:** neighborhood and base housing pages lead with four tiles for your base and pay band. Cost shows your pay band's housing allowance, its rent-to-allowance row and the 3-bedroom rent; "Show all pay bands" has every band. The full allowance table for every grade is on each base page. Commute shows your base first; "All bases" has the rest.
- **Changed:** the notes that used to repeat on every page (how drive times, HUD rent, the rent-to-allowance ratio, the allowance by duty station, school columns and wait ranges work) are now in one place: "How the numbers work" on the Sources page, linked where they apply.
- **Changed:** each move sequence phase starts with a short list of its steps and deadlines. Each step shows its deadline and the forms it uses; the timing, what comes first and the official wording are behind "Details and official wording". The forms page is now a list you can scan, one row per form. The full note on how to read the sequence is on its first page; the other sequence pages carry a one-line version.
- **Changed:** the drive-time groups on base pages use the same amber colors as the map.
- **Fixed:** each neighborhood's Cost section now dates its Mello-Roos line (county list, verified 2026-09-22); a few sections now also show when their outside links were last checked. No figure, wording of a fact or deadline changed.
