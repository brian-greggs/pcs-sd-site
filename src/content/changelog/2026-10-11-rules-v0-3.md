- **Added:** if a child in your household is 2 or younger, the plan's "Watch for" now always notes the gap in infant care: small family child care homes, often where infant care is, aren't in our child care counts, and the county's free referral service is the way to find them. Before, it showed only when child care was your top priority.
- **Changed:** pet rules for base housing now name the community when an area's communities have different rules. Paradise Hills used to read as two policies run together; it's now one line for Bayview Hills and one for Paradise Gardens.
- **Fixed:** each line of the plan now shows only the dates for the facts on that line. Pet lines no longer show a commute date; child care lines, which give drive times, now show the commute date.
- **Why:** from a review of the plan on a phone.
