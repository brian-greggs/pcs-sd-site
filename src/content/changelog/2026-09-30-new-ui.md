- **Changed:** a new look, built for phones first: a dark theme taken from a San Diego sunset, larger tap targets, and every section folds open and shut. The fonts come from this site, not from Google, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else.
- **Added:** the home page starts with the eight bases. Each base page sorts all 40 neighborhoods by the 0700 drive to its main gate, grouped under 30, 30–44, 45–59 and 60+ minutes, and lists the base housing areas tied to it with their latest wait range.
- **Added:** "At a glance" at the top of every neighborhood and base housing page: the 0700 drive to your base (or the nearest), rent against the housing allowance for your pay band, the school district, and fire. Pick your pay band once and the page remembers it on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Added:** an A-to-Z list of all neighborhoods.
