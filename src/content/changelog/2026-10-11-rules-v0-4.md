- **Changed:** "under 30 minutes" in your plan now means the whole drive range is under 30. A neighborhood with zips from 14 to 35 minutes away is listed with the 30–44 minute places, not as under 30. The same goes for "within 45 minutes".
- **Changed:** for Camp Pendleton, the plan no longer lists the base housing areas Liberty Military Housing ties to no base (the Navy-area ones). Whether Pendleton families can live there isn't confirmed, and we'd rather leave them out than list them without saying so.
- **Changed:** the base housing wait warning now fires when even the shortest wait starts at or after your report date. Reporting in under 30 days, a 1–3 month wait doesn't fit, and the plan now says so. It also quotes that one wait ("1–3 months"), not a range across every list.
- **Changed:** the note that base housing allows two pets at most now cites every base housing area it covers, not just the first three.
- **Why:** from a review of the plan on a phone.
