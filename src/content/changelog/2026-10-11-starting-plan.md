- **Added:** a starting plan. Eight questions (where you're reporting, pay grade, when you report, who's moving with you, bedrooms, pets, what you're open to, and your top two priorities), then a plan built by fixed rules from the same data as the rest of the site: one lead for your first priority, what to watch for, and what you can skip. The same answers always give the same plan. Every line names the page it came from and that page's dates, and the plan prints on plain paper.
- **Added:** a "Starting plan for" card on every base page that fills in your base for you, and a link under the base list on the home page. The plan works before you know your base: it shows each neighborhood's two nearest bases.
- **Your answers stay on your device.** They're kept in this browser only, never sent, logged or put in a link, and "Clear my answers" removes them. The plan speaks in rent as a share of the housing allowance, never the allowance in dollars.
- **Not yet:** a lead for "the admin sequence" and "move logistics" (they need the move sequence, which isn't built), the tradeoff between your top two priorities (written from family interviews, pair by pair), and a few rules that point at things the site doesn't have yet. Until then the plan leaves those lines out rather than guessing.
- **Rules:** v0.1, under test. The questions and rules will change after the screener survey and the interviews; each change is logged here with its rules version.
- **Why:** the site is advice, not a link farm. Most families want to know what to look at first for their household, not everything at once.
