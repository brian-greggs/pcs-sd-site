- **New:** a chip at the bottom left of the home page's question box. With no starting-plan answers it says "+ Add your move" and opens the starting plan. Once you've answered, it shows a short summary (your base, pay grade and who's moving with you), and that summary goes with each question you ask, so the answer can use the rows and places that fit you. Tap the chip to see your plan, edit your answers or clear them; tap × to stop sending it, and it stays off in this browser until you turn it back on from the chip. "What's sent?" lists every field that goes.
- **What goes, and what doesn't:** your base, your pay grade, whether a spouse or partner is moving, and your children's age groups (not how many). Never your timeline, bedrooms, pets or priorities. Your answers stay in this browser.
- **Changed:** "Where should we look?" with your base on the chip now starts from your base's closest neighborhoods and its housing allowance table.
- **Changed:** the "Not sure what to ask?" card is gone from the home page, and the map now sits right under the example questions. The starting plan is still in the menu and on the chip.
- No figure, fact, deadline, source or rule changed.
