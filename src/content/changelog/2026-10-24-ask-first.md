- **Changed:** the home page opens with one question box. Type a question, or tap one of the examples, and the answer appears right there, with a small "sources" chip that opens to each page it came from and the date that page was checked, a link to the most relevant page, and a box for a follow-up. If the guide doesn't cover it, the answer says so and points to the nearest page. "What's sent?" next to the box shows exactly what goes with your question. The old /ask/ address now goes to the home page.
- **Changed:** the home page is the same on a phone and a computer: the question box, the examples, "Not sure what to ask?" and the region map, whose neighborhoods and bases still link to their pages. Everything else is in the new menu (top left): the eight bases, neighborhoods, base housing, the move sequence, forms and claims, the starting plan and the sources. On a wide screen the menu is a sidebar you can hide. Without JavaScript, the menu button opens a page with the same links. "San Diego PCS Guide" at the top of every page takes you home; on an answer or the starting plan it says "Start over".
- **Changed:** the starting plan asks one question per screen, with big buttons, a progress bar and Back. Tapping an answer to a one-choice question moves on. The questions, the answers, the plan and where your answers are kept didn't change. The finished plan has "Ask about this plan", which opens the home page's question box with your starting-plan answers ready to include.
- No figure, fact, deadline, source or rule changed.
