title: Base housing areas get their own pages
version: v0.2

- **Added:** 20 base housing area pages. Each lists its Liberty Military Housing communities, the October 2026 wait times by pay grade, pet policy and how to apply, plus the same commute, school, child care, climate and hazard facts as a neighborhood page. The base housing page is now an index of the areas with each one's wait range.
- **Changed:** neighborhood pages no longer carry base housing. The home page lists neighborhoods and base housing areas separately.
- **Changed:** leads and gotchas appear only once they're written; there are no placeholders on any page.
- **Not shown on purpose:** what a family pays for base housing is shown only as the wait list prints it, because the sources don't agree on how rent relates to the housing allowance. Camp Pendleton's wait times aren't in our data yet.
- **Why:** base housing is a different decision from renting or buying nearby, with its own facts: wait times, who can apply, pets and how to apply.
